## About
**ArsenKids** is designed to engage children, toddlers, and families through educational engaging games. It is developed for kids, but also loved by parents alike.

**ArsenKids** teaches **social skills**, strengthens the **bond with parents** through educational videos and games, and stimulates vocabulary and memory. Our videos and games teach children moral values, and these videos are both entertaining and educational. We hope these videos will bring your children, toddlers, and family much joy.

Our stop-motion animated videos are perfectly adapted for their development, and kids enjoy learning new things while watching the videos and our carefully selected playlists. ArsenKids' videos are available in Armenian, but the games are available on all supported languages: Armenian, English, and Russian.
